⚠ Standing Watch List

Peptide line — PCAC recommended 4 key peptides for the 503A bulks list
At the July 23-24 PCAC meeting, the committee voted to recommend BPC-157, KPV, TB-500 (8-6) and MOTS-c (7-5) — plus Semax and Epitalon; it rejected DSIP. Recommendation only: FDA must still issue a final decision (no deadline set), so the injectable peptide line stays in a gray zone for now.
GLP-1 503B bulks exclusion — semaglutide / tirzepatide / liraglutide
Public comment on FDA's proposal to permanently exclude these three from the 503B bulks list closed July 30, 2026. FDA is reviewing comments; no final rule and no announced timeline. Still the highest enforcement exposure in the portfolio.
Estradiol patch shortage — HRT demand surge
Still unresolved: all major brands (Climara, Vivelle-Dot, Teva/Mylan generics) remain backordered; ASHP is tracking it though FDA hasn't officially declared a shortage. Expected through end of 2026, possibly longer. Ongoing opportunity for compounded estradiol cream / troche / capsule / gel.
Testosterone labeling change (TRAVERSE) + indication expansion
FDA issued class-wide testosterone labeling changes — TRAVERSE data added and cardiovascular boxed-warning language removed. Separately, FDA is encouraging sponsors to file sNDAs to expand TRT to low libido in idiopathic hypogonadism. Net tailwind for the TRT line.
Compounded topical finasteride — informed-consent risk
FDA's Apr 2025 adverse-event alert remains in effect (ED, depression, suicidal ideation, brain fog; some persisting after discontinuation). Still no FDA-approved topical labeling. Keep documenting side-effect counseling and pregnancy-transfer warnings.
Methylene blue — serotonin-syndrome boxed warning vs. wellness sales
Boxed warning for fatal serotonin syndrome with SSRIs/SNRIs/MAOIs still stands; FDA treats methylene blue supplements as unapproved new drugs and has issued warning letters over health claims. Counsel patients on concomitant antidepressants.
